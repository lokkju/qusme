Drafts wait in line. You post them.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the extension

    Add qus.me to Chrome or Firefox.

  2. 2

    Create your queue

    The extension makes your queue's key on this device and locks it with your passphrase. It gives you a recovery code and a recovery sheet; keep them somewhere safe, because they get your queue back on a new device.

  3. 3

    Connect Claude

    Add https://app.qus.me/mcp to Claude, approve it in the extension, and choose where it may draft and how many drafts it may add.

  4. 4

    Insert and post

    In a post box, press Alt+Q, pick a draft, read it over and click Post yourself.

Your drafts are yours to read

Encrypted before they leave

Every draft is encrypted with a key only your devices hold. The Nostr relays that store your queue keep data they can't open.

Agents write, they don't read

An agent you approve can drop drafts into your queue. It can't read the queue. You choose its destinations and its limit, and you can revoke it.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, and no selling your data. When an agent submits a draft, app.qus.me sees it only while checking it and doesn't keep it.

Questions

Does qus.me post for me?

No. It puts the draft you pick into the post box. You read it, edit it if you want, and click Post. qus.me never posts.

Can the agent read my drafts?

No. Agents can add drafts to your queue, but they can't read it. The queue is encrypted with a key only your devices hold.

What sites work?

Sites with a post box. X is built in, and you can add others, like LinkedIn or Facebook, as your own destinations. Alt+Q opens the picker in the field you're typing in.

What if I lose my device?

Use the recovery code or recovery sheet you saved when you created the queue, or pair the new device from another one you already set up. Without the code and with no other device, the queue can't be recovered; nobody else holds its key.

What does it cost?

It's free while in beta.

How it's built

Your queue is a Nostr key pair made in the extension. Drafts are encrypted end to end before they're published to Nostr relays, so relays hold only ciphertext. Agents reach your queue through the MCP server at app.qus.me after you approve them with OAuth; the server seals their drafts to your queue's inbox and can't read the queue itself. The code is open source on GitHub.
